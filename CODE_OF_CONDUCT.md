# Code of Conduct

Inglen is built in the open, and people of every background and skill level use it and work on it. Everyone taking part in its issues, pull requests and other project spaces is expected to treat each other with respect.

## What's expected

- Be kind and assume good faith.
- Disagree about the work, not the person.
- Give feedback that helps someone do better.
- Accept correction, and own your mistakes.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery aimed at anyone.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or filing low-effort reports and pull requests in bulk.

## Reporting

Write to [hello@inglen.app](mailto:hello@inglen.app) with "Conduct" in the subject. Reports are read privately and answered as soon as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block an account from the project, depending on how serious the behavior is and whether it continues.

This applies in project spaces, and in public when someone is representing the project.
